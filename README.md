## Featured project

**[reddit-speedskaters-genderanalysis](https://github.com/tessdg/reddit-speedskaters-genderanalysis)**
Bachelor's thesis (Erasmus School of Social and Behavioural Sciences, grade 8.8/10):
tested whether Reddit comments about Dutch elite speed skaters differ by gender
in whether they focus on the athlete as a person or on their performance.
Full pipeline from scraping to a locally hosted LLM classifier (Qwen2.5 via
Ollama), multilingual sentiment analysis (XLM-RoBERTa), and hypothesis testing,
with both models validated against manually labelled samples.
